Operational Purpose: This field manual provides step-by-step guidance for security officers conducting farm day & night patrols, gate vehicle logging, emergency SOS alerts, and shift summaries.

1. Guard Shift Procedure: Start to Finish

Step 1: Clock-In with Attendance Selfie

  1. Open Eagle Eye from your phone home screen.
  2. Select your name from the guard roster (e.g. Wag 1 / Sipho Khoza).
  3. Tap the large blue button: START SHIFT (SELFIE).
  4. Look into the camera, ensure good light, and take your clock-in selfie.
  5. Tap Confirm Photo. The duty timer begins immediately.

Step 2: Conducting Patrol Rounds

  1. Check your home screen for the Next Patrol Round countdown.
  2. Tap the Patrol tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Walk to each physical checkpoint beacon around the farm.
  4. If scanning an NFC tag: Tap "Tap NFC Tag" and hold the back of your phone against the round plastic tag.
  5. If scanning a QR card: Tap "Scan QR Card" and point the camera at the laminated checkpoint card (turn on torch in low light).
  6. Wait for the vibration and confirmation card: ✓ CHECKPOINT VERIFIED.

Step 3: Gate Access & Licence-Disc Scanning

  1. Tap the Gate tab at the bottom of your screen.
  2. Tap "Scan Licence Disc".
  3. Point the camera at the rectangular barcode on the vehicle's licence disc.
  4. Check the decoded plate, make, colour, and expiry date.
  5. Tap "Confirm Information" and tap "Log Vehicle".
  6. Vehicle Exit: Switch toggle to Vehicle OUT, tap the leaving vehicle, and tap Record Exit.

Step 4: Reporting an Incident

  1. Tap the Incident tab.
  2. Select what happened (e.g. Fence cut, Gate open, Livestock loose, Intruder, Fire).
  3. Type a short description and tap "Add Photograph" to take evidence photos.
  4. Tap "Submit Incident Report" (GPS location is tagged automatically).

Step 5: Clock-Out & WhatsApp Summary

  1. At the end of your shift, tap "End Shift (Selfie)" on the home screen.
  2. Take your clock-out selfie and confirm.
  3. The Shift Completed screen will display your round statistics.
  4. Tap "Send Summary to WhatsApp" to send the official report to the supervisor.

2. Emergency SOS Panic Alarm

CRITICAL EMERGENCY PROCEDURE: In case of armed intruders, assault, or immediate danger, locate the red SOS PANIC button.
  • Press and hold the button for 2 full seconds. The progress ring will fill and the phone will vibrate.
  • Let go to dispatch. Your live GPS coordinates are sent to control and supervisor.
  • Tap "Call Supervisor" for immediate voice support, or "Call Police (10111)" for SAPS tactical backup.

3. Hardware & Sensor Troubleshooting Matrix

Sensor / Feature Common Issue Resolution Step
Camera "Camera access was blocked" Open phone Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Camera → Select "Allow while using app".
GPS / Location "GPS signal not available" / weak accuracy Turn on phone Location. Enable Google Location Accuracy (Precise) and step outdoors into the open air.
Web NFC Tag not reading / no reaction Verify NFC is switched ON in Settings → Connections → NFC. If tag is 125 kHz RFID button or phone is an iPhone, scan the QR code instead.
Offline Mode No cellular reception / data out Keep patrolling. Eagle Eye saves all scans safely in phone memory (IndexedDB) and auto-syncs when signal returns.
Patrol Alarm Phone beeps triple tone & vibrates The current patrol round is overdue by >10 minutes. Begin your round immediately to clear the alarm.
